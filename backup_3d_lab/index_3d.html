COMPUTER LAB GUIDE & CONTROLS

🎮 Navigation & Character

  • W, A, S, D : Walk forward, left, backward, right
  • Left Shift : Sprint / run with dynamic body tilt
  • Space : Jump into the air
  • E : Sit down on any student or teacher chair (triggers typing animation!) or stand up
  • Mouse : Look and rotate camera
  • Mouse Wheel : Zoom in / out in Third-Person mode
  • V : Cycle camera modes: Third-Person Follow ➜ First-Person ➜ Free Orbit
  • C : Open live Character Customization Panel
  • ESC : Unlock mouse cursor

🖥️ Interactive Equipment

  • Chairs: Walk near any chair and press E to sit and type!
  • Teacher Smart Board: Click the screen to cycle lecture presentation slides
  • Window Blinds: Click window frames or blinds to raise/lower Venetian blinds
  • Server Rack: Click to inspect network cluster and switch activity
  • 3D Printer & Scanner: Click to inspect print and scanning queues

💡 Environment Controls

  • T : Switch between daytime natural sunlight & night lab mode
  • H : Toggle this help reference guide
